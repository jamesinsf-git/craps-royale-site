Getting Started
What is Craps Royale?+

A full-featured craps simulator for iPhone and Android. Learn the table, practice betting strategies, and feel the energy of the game, without risking a dime. All chips and wagers are virtual.

How do I place a bet?+

Tap a chip denomination to select it, then tap the spot on the table you want to bet on. You can place as many bets as you like before each roll. Long-press any bet to remove it (some bets like Pass Line after the point can't be removed; that follows real casino rules).

How do I throw the dice?+

Drag a die toward the back wall and let go to flick it. The ROLL button works too. If you prefer the classic look, you can turn 3D dice off any time under Settings, Dice.

Chips & Progress
My chip balance ran out, what now?+

When you run out of chips, a rebuy pops up automatically to top you back up so you can keep rolling. The free rebuy is available anytime; the High Roller pack adds a bigger $2K rebuy.

If you'd rather start completely fresh, Settings has a Reset Game option, but note that it clears your chip balance and your XP, level, and achievements. Your purchases and settings are untouched. It can't be undone.

Purchases
Is Craps Royale free?+

Yes. Free to download with full access to the core game. The free version shows ads between sessions. Two optional purchases are available:

  • Ad-Free Experience: removes all ads permanently.
  • High Roller Pack: unlocks Crapless Craps, the exact-amount payout calculator, premium themes, high-denomination chips, extra strategies, a bigger rebuy, and a 2× XP boost.
How do I restore a purchase on a new device?+

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. On iPhone, be signed in with the same Apple ID that made the original purchase; on Android, the same Google Account. Purchases don't transfer between the two platforms.

Can I get a refund?+

Refunds are handled by whichever store sold you the app:

  • iPhone (App Store): visit reportaproblem.apple.com, find the Craps Royale purchase, and request a refund from Apple.
  • Android (Google Play): visit play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory and request a refund from Google. Purchases made within 48 hours are typically eligible for a self-service refund.
Troubleshooting
The app crashed+

Force-quit and re-open the app. If a specific action causes a repeat crash, email us with your device model and OS version (iOS or Android). That's the fastest way to get it fixed.

The dice are slow or stuttering+

3D dice run a real physics simulation. On older devices you can switch to classic 2D dice under Settings, Dice, which runs smoothly on anything. Closing other apps running in the background can also help.

A bet didn't pay out correctly+

Craps payouts vary by bet. In the app, tap the ? on any bet to see its exact payout and house edge, which is the fastest way to double-check. If you still think there's a bug, email us with the bet type and roll outcome and we'll investigate.
