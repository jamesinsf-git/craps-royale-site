PRIVACY
POLICY

Last updated: June 2026

Craps Royale is a casino-style craps simulator for entertainment purposes only. No real money is involved. This policy explains what data the app does and does not collect.

◆

We don't collect personal information

No name, no email, no location. We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

What we don't collect

Craps Royale does not collect personal information. We do not track your name, email address, location, or any other identifying details. We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Advertising

The free version of Craps Royale displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers (such as the IDFA on iOS, subject to your App Tracking Transparency choice) to serve relevant ads. You can limit ad tracking under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device. Purchasing the Ad-Free Experience in-app purchase permanently removes all ads. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Data that stays on your device

Your chip balance, game history, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device. None of this is transmitted to us or to any third party.

No real money

Craps Royale is strictly a simulator. All chips and wagers are virtual and have no monetary value. The app does not facilitate real-money gambling of any kind.

In-app purchases

Craps Royale offers two optional in-app purchases: Ad-Free Experience (removes all ads) and High Roller Pack (unlocks premium themes, high-denomination chips, and XP boost). All transactions are processed by Apple via the App Store. We use RevenueCat to verify purchase status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous device identifier — no personal information — solely to confirm whether a purchase has been made. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, your device may send anonymized crash diagnostics to Apple, depending on your device-level settings. You can manage this under Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. We do not run any crash reporting service of our own.

Children's privacy

Craps Royale is intended for users aged 17 and older in accordance with App Store guidelines for simulated gambling apps. The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date. Checking this page is the best way to stay informed.
